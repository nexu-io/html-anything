Keep only guidance with a durable owner and maintenance purpose. Plans, migration coverage, acceptance records, logs and temporary research belong in PRs/issues and delivery records, tied to exact source and evidence. Do not disguise a process archive as architecture. Design constraints describe intended boundaries; implemented support is established by the actual source and the operations guide.
