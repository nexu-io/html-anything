# Motion MD — Product reveal

- Format: Motion MD draft v0.1

## Brand & assets

Use the product owner's approved logo, UI screenshots, type and color palette. Link the actual brand document when available. Product screens must remain legible and accurate; do not invent features or customer claims.

Expected inputs: logo, two screenshots, approved headline, two benefits and final CTA. Their filenames and permissions must be supplied with the project; this example does not provide or license them.

## Audience & promise

Audience: small product teams preparing their next launch.

Message: bring the team's next steps into one clear plan.

Illustrative headline: “Your next launch, in focus.” Replace this with approved product copy before production.

## Story & timing

Proposed output: 1920 × 1080, 16:9, 30 fps, 20 seconds. This is an example brief, not the required format for all Motion MD files.

| Time | Purpose | Picture & copy | Transition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Establish a recognizable problem | A few fragmented task cards; one short problem statement. | Cards settle into a shared canvas. |
| 3–8 s | Introduce the product | Reveal the owner's logo and product headline, followed by the first real screenshot. | Short eased move; preserve the central focal point. |
| 8–16 s | Make two benefits concrete | Use the two real screenshots and one approved benefit for each. Hold each view long enough to read. | Move focus between features without hiding the product context. |
| 16–20 s | Leave a clear action | Product name and one CTA; keep the closing frame readable. | Settle into a clean final composition. |

## Motion & composition

- Use a shared visual canvas and clear text hierarchy.
- Prefer short eased movements with a readable hold after each reveal.
- Animate to guide attention; avoid constant motion behind small text.
- Preserve logo proportions and product UI geometry.
- Review the opening and final frame independently so both read clearly as stills.

These are authored creative choices, not measurements extracted from a reference.
